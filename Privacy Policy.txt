Privacy Policy for Countdown: Days Left
Effective date: October 7, 2026
Developer: LosaStudio
LosaStudio provides Countdown: Days Left. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us.
1. Information Stored on Your Device
The app stores information you enter locally on your device, including:
- Event titles and target dates or times.
- Selected event colors and icons.
- Favorite events and past events.
- Display settings and theme preferences.
This information is used to display countdowns, organize events, and restore your saved preferences. We do not receive this information or store it on developer servers.
The app does not require an account.
2. Advertising and Analytics
The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect advertising identifiers or track your activity across other apps.
We do not sell or rent your information.
3. Sharing Events
When you choose to share an event, the app sends its title, date, and countdown information to the application or recipient you select through your device’s sharing options.
Sharing occurs only when you initiate it. The receiving service handles shared information according to its own privacy policy.
4. Permissions and Device Information
The app uses your device’s current date, time, and local time zone to calculate countdowns.
Its countdown features do not require access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or personal calendar.
5. Retention and Deletion
Your events and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or clear the app’s storage.
You can delete individual events through the app. To remove all locally stored information, use the app’s clear-data option, where available, or clear its storage through Android Settings.
Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data. We cannot access or delete your saved events remotely because we do not receive them.
6. Device Backups
Depending on your device settings and the app’s backup configuration, Android or your device provider may include app data in backups.
We do not operate or have access to these backups. Backup retention, deletion, and restoration are controlled by your backup provider and device settings.
7. Support Communications
If you contact us by email or phone, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your contact details, message, and attachments.
We use this information to respond to questions and resolve support issues. Our email and telecommunications providers may process these communications to deliver their services.
Support communications are retained only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to request their deletion.
8. Security
The app relies on Android’s app storage protections and your device’s security settings to protect locally stored information.
Keep your device secure and updated. No method of electronic storage or communication can guarantee absolute security.
9. Children’s Privacy
The app does not require users to submit personal information to LosaStudio. Event information entered into the app remains on the device.
If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.
10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy when the app or its information-handling practices change. The updated policy will display a revised effective date.
